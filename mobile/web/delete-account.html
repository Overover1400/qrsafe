Delete your QRSafe account

QRSafe, by Semicolon · last updated 26 November 2026

This page explains how to have your QRSafe account and the data attached to it deleted. QRSafe is published by Semicolon.

How to ask us to delete it

  1. Email info@semicolon.live with the subject Delete my QRSafe account.
  2. Send it from the email address you signed in with. If you never signed in, write from any address and tell us the code you want removed, or ask us to delete the anonymous account that made it.
  3. We reply to confirm, and the deletion is done within 30 days.

Email us to delete my account

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and the email address you signed in with Deleted
Every QR code you saved, with its contents and label Deleted
Your short links and dynamic codes Deleted. A code already printed will stop working

What we keep, and for how long

  • Scan records for short links — the time of a scan, the browser user-agent and a one-way hash of the visitor IP address. These are kept for up to 12 months and then removed. They carry no personal identifier and are not linked back to you once the account is gone.
  • Nothing else is kept.

Deleting one code instead of the account

You do not have to delete everything. Delete a single code inside the app and it is removed from our server at the same time. You can also ask us by email to delete particular codes and keep the account.

Your phone

Photos and PDFs from the document scanner are stored on your own device and were never sent to us, so deleting your account does not touch them. Remove them from your phone yourself.

Full details of what QRSafe stores are in the privacy policy.
